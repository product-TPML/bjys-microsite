QUICK FACTS

The scheme in 9 cards

01 / 09

01 10,000

Yuva Sanghas across Karnataka

  • Gram Panchayat1 per GP
  • Town Panchayat1 per 4,000 people
  • Municipality1 per 6,000 people
  • City Municipal Council1 per 8,000 people
  • Corporation / GBA1 per 10,000 people
02 16–35

Your age to join

Age is counted as on 15 August 2026, as checked by the official portal during registration.

03 Your area.
Your Sangha.

Join where you live

Open to Karnataka residents. You join the Sangha of the Gram Panchayat or urban ward where you ordinarily reside.

04 1

Sangha per person

You can be a member of only one Sangha.

05 100–250

Members in every Sangha

Each Sangha is run by a 15-member Executive Committee.

06 ₹10 lakh

Every year, for every Sangha

An annual grant to each approved Sangha for youth programmes and community activities. The grant goes to the Sangha, not to individual members.

07 Pick 3

from 8 areas of interest

  • Sports & Fitness
  • Yoga & Wellness
  • Cultural Activities
  • Environment & Tree Plantation
  • Teaching
  • Community Service
  • Disaster Management
  • Leadership & Personality Development
08 ₹10

One-time registration fee

Same fee online or at a service centre. All you need is your Aadhaar (for e-KYC) and a mobile number (for OTP). No separate document upload.

09 7 steps

to your Membership Card

  1. Verify mobile
  2. Aadhaar e-KYC
  3. Address
  4. Join your Sangha
  5. Pick interests
  6. Pay ₹10
  7. Download card
